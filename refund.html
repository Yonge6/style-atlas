Web purchases through Paddle

When web checkout launches, Paddle will be the merchant of record. Refund eligibility, statutory withdrawal rights, processing, and payment-method limitations are governed by Paddle's Refund Policy and applicable consumer law. Where either provides stronger non-waivable rights, those rights apply.

To cancel an auto-renewing subscription or request a refund, use the “Manage subscription” or receipt link in your Paddle confirmation email, or visit paddle.net. Cancelling stops future renewal; access ordinarily continues until the end of the paid period.

Technical problems

If a persistent technical problem materially prevents access to paid features, contact hustyy986@gmail.com with the purchase email, order reference, device/browser details, and steps to reproduce. We will first try to restore access. If the problem cannot be resolved, we will support an eligible refund through Paddle rather than sending money outside Paddle.

iPhone purchases

Purchases made through Apple In-App Purchase are billed and refunded by Apple. Requests must be submitted through Apple's purchase-history or support process and are subject to Apple's policies and applicable law.

No reduction of mandatory rights

Digital access that has already been used may not be refundable except where required by law or approved under the payment provider's policy. Nothing here removes mandatory consumer rights.
