Who we are

Style Atlas: Art & Design is a visual learning product operated by Yong Yuan under the WonderElian name. Contact: hustyy986@gmail.com.

Website data

The public website does not currently require an account. Favorites and reflections created in the current web experience are stored locally in your browser unless a feature clearly states otherwise.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage and App Store link activity. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled. Analytics may process technical information such as page paths, device and browser details, approximate region, referral information, and interaction events. We do not sell personal data or use third-party advertising.

Purchases

iPhone purchases are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment credentials and transaction records; the app reads the Apple-verified entitlement needed to provide access.

When web checkout becomes available, Paddle will act as merchant of record and payment processor. Paddle will process payment, tax, billing, and transaction information under its own privacy notice. Style Atlas will receive only the customer and transaction information needed to provide access, support the purchase, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. No web checkout is active before Paddle approves the account.

Retention and rights

Local browser data can be removed by clearing the relevant feature or browser storage. Transaction and support records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for access, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and legal compliance. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion rights that apply in your jurisdiction.

Changes

We will update this policy before materially changing how personal data is collected or used.
