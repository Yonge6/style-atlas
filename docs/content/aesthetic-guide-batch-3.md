## Fact And Context Review

- De Stijl was checked as a movement around the journal and shared abstraction, not a synonym for Mondrian.
- Futurism copy includes dynamic movement, force and typography while keeping its political history restrained and non-celebratory.
- Graphic Brutalism is framed as a contemporary graphic borrowing of exposed, unpolished structure, separate from architectural Brutalism.
- New Wave Typography and Experimental Typography remain distinct: the former is a postmodern graphic-design context; the latter covers broader letterform, material, spatial and dynamic experiments.
- Punk DIY and Grunge remain distinct through production method versus eroded surface and 1990s music-culture texture.
- Psychedelic Poster avoids drug romanticization or guidance.
- Corporate Modernism is presented as a reproducible institutional system, not a luxury brand recipe.
- Conceptual Art is written around idea, language, record and institution, not "anything is art."
- Dalí and Magritte are both separated from Surrealism as a movement and from each other as individual visual languages.
- Hopper avoids fixed emotional claims about sadness.
- Pollock is separated from Abstract Expressionism and Color Field Painting; the text avoids the "mad genius" myth.
- Basquiat copy keeps words, signs, Black history, Haitian and Puerto Rican family background, New York street/gallery context and art-market tension without turning symbols into a dictionary.
