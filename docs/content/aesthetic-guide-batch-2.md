## Fact And Context Review

- Renaissance perspective, human-scale proportion and modeled anatomy were checked against the Metropolitan Museum's Renaissance teaching and Timeline materials.
- Neoclassicism is described as an eighteenth-century revival shaped by archaeology and classical precedent, not as a continuation label for all Renaissance art.
- Cézanne and Cubism are linked through shifting viewpoints, solidity, color and constructed space; Picasso is consistently described as one important Cubist participant.
- Expressionism is the broad category. German Expressionism is a more specific historical and regional field involving groups such as Die Brücke and Der Blaue Reiter.
- Abstract Expressionism includes gestural and color-field directions. It is not used as a synonym for Pollock.
- Color Field Painting is treated as a broader direction containing Rothko, Newman, Frankenthaler, Gilliam and other distinct approaches.
- Symbolism entries explicitly require work-specific context instead of a fixed motif dictionary.
- Dada's use of readymades, collage, chance, language and collaboration is connected to institutional and wartime critique.
- No artist quotations, dates, biographies, fixed spiritual claims or universal emotional responses were invented.
