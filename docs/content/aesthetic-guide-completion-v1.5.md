## Editorial Boundaries

- Every added Guide contains a bilingual opening question, three observations, four Profile traits, four feeling words, four Everyday scenes, two valid Comparisons and one Reflection prompt.
- North American Indigenous Art is not described as one universal style. The Guide directs readers to specific nations, communities, makers, materials and cultural protocols.
- Afrofuturism is grounded in Black histories and African diasporic experience; it is not reduced to generic African pattern plus science fiction.
- Tibetan thangka and Byzantine icons are presented as culturally and religiously situated practices, not interchangeable decoration.
- Contemporary animation and AI-related entries describe broad visual languages without instructing users to copy a living creator, studio or protected character.
