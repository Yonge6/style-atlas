## Language And Storefront Baseline

- A fresh install opens in English, independent of the device locale; users can switch to Chinese and the choice is then retained.
- United States is the primary storefront and U.S. English is the primary App Store localization for the next upload.
- App name, subtitle, promotional text, description, keywords, screenshots, subscription display names, and review notes must be reviewed in U.S. English before upload.
- Chinese remains an available in-app language and a separate App Store localization; it must not replace or be inherited by the U.S. English metadata.

## Territory Pricing

Configure the United States as the base storefront for both new products.

- Monthly auto-renewing base price: USD 9.99.
- Annual auto-renewing base price: USD 89.99.
- Annual introductory offer: free for 7 days for eligible new subscribers.
- Other storefronts: use App Store Connect automatic local-currency equivalents from the United States base price, then review the generated price schedule before submission.
- The app must display StoreKit `displayPrice`; it must not calculate exchange rates or hard-code a storefront currency.

## Review Requirements

- The annual plan states its one-year period, total annual price, trial eligibility, post-trial billing, automatic renewal, cancellation path, Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy.
- The monthly plan states its one-month period, total monthly price, automatic renewal, and cancellation path.
- Restore Purchases covers active monthly or annual access and both legacy entitlement types.
- A verified but expired or revoked transaction does not unlock Plus.
- Sandbox testing is required for purchase, cancellation, expiry, renewal, restore, upgrade from a free install, and legacy lifetime migration.
