## Review Requirements

- The annual auto-renewing plan states its one-year period, total annual price, automatic renewal, cancellation path, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
- The one-year pass states that it expires after one year and does not renew automatically.
- Restore Purchases covers active annual access and legacy lifetime access. The non-renewing pass must be tested on a second device because the app is responsible for restoring its access period.
- A verified but expired or revoked transaction does not unlock Plus.
- Sandbox testing is required for purchase, cancellation, expiry, renewal, restore, upgrade from a free install, and legacy lifetime migration.
