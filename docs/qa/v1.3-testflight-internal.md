Severity rules:

- `Blocker`: cannot launch, data loss, purchase or restore completely unavailable, export completely unavailable, many blank pages, or a serious crash.
- `High`: a repeatable core-flow failure, important content unreachable, incorrect Plus entitlement, Reflection cannot save, or severe key-page layout failure.

- `Low`: minor copy, spacing or visual preference.

An RC2 may be proposed only for a Blocker, High, release-impacting Medium, or App Review blocker. Low issues do not create RC2.
